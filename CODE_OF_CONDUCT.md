# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to build and use Hive. Everyone who
takes part in its issues, pull requests and any other place the project
keeps is asked to follow these few rules.

## What we ask

- **Be respectful.** Talk about the work, not about the person. Disagree
  plainly and kindly.
- **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes are mistakes. Ask before you assume
  a bad reason, and say so when you got something wrong yourself.
- **Be patient with newcomers.** A question that has been asked before is
  still a fair question. Point to the answer.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and pull requests are for Hive.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment of any kind: insults, threats, unwelcome personal or sexual
  attention, or following someone from thread to thread.
- Attacks on someone for who they are: their gender, origin, religion, age,
  body, disability, sexuality, or level of experience.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Keeping on after you were asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here is a problem, tell the maintainers. Use the
report option in the menu of the comment, issue or pull request, or get in
touch with a maintainer named in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS)
through their GitHub profile. Say what happened and where, with links.
Reports are kept in confidence.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests
that break these rules, lock a thread, and block a person from the project
for a time or for good. They decide case by case and they say why.

## Where this comes from

This page follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). It is written
in our own words and kept short. Where it is silent, that document is a good
guide.
